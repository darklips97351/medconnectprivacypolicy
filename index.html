Privacy Policy

Namaz & Hadees

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Namaz & Hadees mobile application (“the app”) handles information when you use it. The app provides prayer times for a city you choose, optional prayer and Hadith notifications, and advertising.

Information you choose to provide

The app does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or payment information. You choose a country and city in the app. The app does not request your device’s GPS location.

Your selected country and city, prayer calculation preferences, Jamaat times, and notification settings are saved on your device so the app can remember your choices and schedule reminders.

Information sent to service providers

To provide city search and prayer times, the app sends limited request information to these services:

  • Open-Meteo geocoding receives the city search text and selected country code to find matching cities.
  • AlAdhan receives the selected city’s latitude and longitude, time zone, prayer calculation method, and Asr setting to calculate prayer times.
  • A country-name list is downloaded from GitHub.

These requests are made over the internet. The services may receive your device’s IP address and standard request information, and handle it under their own privacy policies. The app does not send your saved Jamaat times or notification settings to these services.

Notifications

If you grant notification permissions, the app schedules prayer and daily Hadith reminders locally on your device. These reminders are not sent through a push-notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can change reminder settings in the app and revoke notification permissions in your device settings.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as device identifiers, IP address, device information, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads, limit fraud, and provide related advertising services. Google's handling of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Ad personalization and consent choices may depend on your region, device, and Google settings.

How long information is kept

App preferences are stored on your device until you change or clear them, or uninstall the app. Scheduled notifications are managed by your device. Information sent to third-party services is subject to those providers’ retention practices and policies.

Children’s privacy

The app does not require an account or ask users to submit personal information. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of apps that display third-party advertising.

Security and your choices

You can change or remove saved location and reminder preferences in the app, turn off notifications in the app or device settings, or uninstall the app to remove its locally stored preferences. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is made.
